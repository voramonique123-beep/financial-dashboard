Important limitations

ROCE is calculated using statutory operating profit and capital employed, not the companies' adjusted ROCE definitions. Inventory turnover uses closing inventory; other working-capital calculations also use closing balances. M&S's 2026 comparison is affected by Ocado Retail consolidation and cyber-related disruption. Sainsbury's results may include restatements and discontinued operations.

Check all inputs and formula definitions against the original annual reports before coursework submission.
