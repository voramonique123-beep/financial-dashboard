Executive interpretation

M&S's 2026 reported revenue increased, but statutory operating margin and group-calculated ROCE weakened. Sainsbury's margins were lower but steadier. Sources, methodology and limitations

All ratios for 2023–2026 are calculated directly from the uploaded group Excel workbook, “AFAR_Group Persentation_Formulas - Copy(1).xlsx”, sheet “Verified Inputs”. The underlying values and formula definitions are the group’s coursework inputs and must be checked by the group against the annual reports before submission.

  • Marks and Spencer Group plc — Full Year Results 2026 (company-reported financial context).
  • J Sainsbury plc — Annual Report 2026 (company-reported financial context).
  • J Sainsbury plc — Interim Results 2026/27 (outlook context).

Ratio formulas: Gross margin = gross profit / revenue; operating margin = statutory operating profit / revenue; ROCE = operating profit / capital employed; net asset turnover = revenue / capital employed; inventory turnover = cost of sales / closing inventory; receivables days = closing trade receivables / revenue × 365; payables days = closing trade payables / cost of sales × 365; current ratio = current assets / current liabilities; quick ratio = (current assets − inventories) / current liabilities; gearing = debt including leases / (equity + debt) × 100; interest cover = operating profit / finance costs. All use closing balance sheet values, not averages.

Comparability: M&S consolidated Ocado Retail in FY2026; Sainsbury's comparative results include restatements and discontinued operations. Company-defined adjusted ROCE is not the same as the group's workbook ROCE. Inventory turnover is expressed in times, not days.

Before submission: All four years are populated. Verify workbook inputs, formula choices, and any restatements against the original annual reports before submitting.
